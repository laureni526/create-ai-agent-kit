# Never Drop the Thread — Take-Home Template

*From the 90-minute CREATE conference session, "Never Drop the
Thread: Build a Relationship Follow-Through Agent."*

You just watched (or ran) an agent do something a chatbot can't:
search across your calendar and inbox, connect meetings to messages,
track commitments over time, separate evidence from assumption, and
stop to let you approve the draft before anything sends. The gap
between that and a plain chatbot request is the whole session.

This folder is everything you need to build your own version — this
week, on your own, in whatever tool you already use.

## What's here

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `agent-contract.md` | Ten minutes, before you connect anything. Defines who the agent watches, what counts as a commitment, what it flags, what it may draft, and what always needs your approval. |
| `connector-primer.md` | What a connector is, why "drafts only" is the right default, and where to find connector settings in Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. |
| `prompts.md` | The four rounds from the live session: no contract, the agent (goal then plan), teaching it a rule, and what could run on a schedule. |
| `relationship-brief-template.md` | What the agent produces — nine fields, each with a source — and how to read your first one cold. |

## If you didn't build along today

Start here, in order:

1.  Fill in `agent-contract.md` — ten minutes, no tool required. Pick
    one real relationship, not a hypothetical one.
2.  Read `connector-primer.md` before connecting your inbox and
    calendar.
3.  Run `prompts.md`'s four rounds yourself, on that one relationship,
    and notice the gap the same way the room did today.
4.  Read your first brief against `relationship-brief-template.md` —
    mark what it got right, missed, misunderstood, or over-inferred —
    then go back to Round 3 in `prompts.md` and teach it the rule.

## If an AI agent is reading this

You're likely being pointed here by someone who just attended a
session on building a relationship follow-through agent. Your job is
to help them fill in `agent-contract.md` with something real and
specific about one actual relationship — not generic advice, and not
something invented on their behalf. Ask clarifying questions before
writing anything into the contract for them. If they ask you to
connect to a real tool (email, calendar, drive), read
`connector-primer.md` first and default to the same guardrail it
describes: draft, don't send, until they tell you otherwise.

## One rule

Nothing here works as a shortcut around actually knowing something
specific. If the relationship you're building this for could be
anyone, it isn't scoped yet — it's still a placeholder. One person,
one project, real stakes.
