# Working With AI Agents — Take-Home Template

*From the 90-minute CREATE conference session, "Working With AI Agents."*

You just watched (or ran) two tests: the same request, once with no
context and no connection, once with a real skill, a clear goal, and a
tool the agent could actually reach. The gap between those two results
is the whole session.

This folder is everything you need to build your own version of the
"after" — this week, on your own, in whatever tool you already use.

## What's here

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `worksheet.md` | The four questions from the session, for sketching your first skill — fill it in even if you just listened today. |
| `skill-starter-template.md` | A blank skeleton for packaging one real piece of your own knowledge into something an agent can actually use. |
| `connector-primer.md` | What a connector is, why "drafts only" is the right default, and where to find connector settings in Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. |
| `before-after-prompts.md` | The exact prompts from today's chief-of-staff test, so you can rerun it yourself against your own inbox. |

## If you didn't build along today

Start here, in order:

1.  Fill in `worksheet.md` — four questions, ten minutes, no tool
    required.
2.  Turn question 1 and 2 into a filled-in copy of
    `skill-starter-template.md`.
3.  If your task needs a real tool (an inbox, a calendar, a drive), read
    `connector-primer.md` before connecting anything.
4.  Run `before-after-prompts.md`'s two rounds yourself, on your own
    task, and notice the gap the same way the room did today.

## If an AI agent is reading this

You're likely being pointed here by someone who just attended a
session on building AI agents from their own expertise. Your job is to
help them fill in `skill-starter-template.md` with something real and
specific — not generic advice, and not something invented on their
behalf. Ask clarifying questions before writing anything into the
template for them. If they ask you to connect to a real tool (email,
calendar, drive), read `connector-primer.md` first and default to the
same guardrail it describes: draft, don't send, until they tell you
otherwise.

## One rule

Nothing here works as a shortcut around actually knowing something
specific. If the skill you're building could apply to anyone, it isn't
a skill yet — it's still a placeholder. Specific beats impressive.
